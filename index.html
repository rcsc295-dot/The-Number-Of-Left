    Instructions

    MOVEWASD or clickTAKEJGIVEK

    Each person carries a number showing how many encounters remain.

    J takes one encounter from an unprotected person. Blood relatives and letter targets can receive time, but cannot be taken from.

    K gives one stored encounter.

    When someone’s number reaches 0, they will no longer return.

    A successful K · Give that raises a stranger’s number to exactly 5 establishes a friendship. It remains a friendship as their number changes. Spawning at 5 does not count. Every four gifts separately renews temporary following.

    Some things are hidden.

    Click to move; WASD immediately takes over. Clicking never takes or gives.
    Release J or K before pressing again.
    Use ⏸ to pause, ▶ to resume, and ? for these instructions.

    Character and world architecture informed by Nicky Case’s We Become What We Behold: independent walkers, layered figures, depth ordering, and staged scenes.

    Conceptual inspiration: Friedrich Nietzsche — Eternal Recurrence; Henri Poincaré — Poincaré Recurrence.

    Original character drawings and typographic animation. Walking hop and spring animation adapted from Nicky Case’s We Become What We Behold (CC0 1.0). SVG.js 3.2.5 is bundled under the MIT licence. The adapted animation comes from the pinned Peep.js source. The hop and spring run inside the existing delta-time update; no WBWWB artwork or audio is included.
